## Engineering Approach

- Make a small change, run the targeted verification gate, and leave the repository commit-ready.
- Treat generated output, salvage material, and canonical source as separate classes.
- Prefer machine-readable reports and manifests before destructive cleanup or promotion.
- Use independent or negative-control verification when a runtime-enforcement change is important enough to require stronger evidence.
